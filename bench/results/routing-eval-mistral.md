Generated by `bench/routing_eval.py` from `bench/data/routing_runs.jsonl` (collected by `bench/routing_collect.py`, local Ollama, $0). 60 prompts with reference answers, half easy and half hard. Cheap = `llama3.2:1b`, strong = `mistral:latest`. Grading compares against the reference; no LLM grades the results.

Cost is the real token counts at small/large-model list-style prices (cheap $0.15/$0.60, strong $1.05/$4.20 per Mtok in/out, i.e. in proportion to parameter count) and is shown relative to always-strong. Brackets are 95% bootstrap intervals over prompts: with 60 prompts, differences under ~10 points are noise.

## Which check won

The cheap model was wrong on 11 of 60 prompts. A check *fires* when it escalates. It is *right to fire* when the cheap answer was wrong. It is *useful* when the strong model then got it right.

## Notes

- By difficulty: cheap 28/30 easy, 21/30 hard; strong 29/30 easy, 19/30 hard.
- The 1B judge said NO to 10 of 11 wrong answers but also to 42 of 49 correct ones (0 verdicts unparseable): it barely discriminates, so escalating on its NO is close to escalating everything.
- Logprobs (the plan's confidence signal) aren't exposed by this Ollama version, so self-consistency stands in: 3 extra samples at temperature 0.7.
- Latency is sequential wall time on one M2 laptop, including model swaps. Read it as relative only.
- `heuristics + format` uses this eval's 'Final answer:' convention, which the gateway can't know in general, so it is not a production check.
