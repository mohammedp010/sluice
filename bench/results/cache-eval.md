# Semantic cache: threshold choice and false-hit rate

Generated by `bench/cache_eval.py` (local Ollama embeddings, $0) from `bench/data/cache_pairs.jsonl`: 160 labelled pairs, 81 dev / 79 test.

> **Caveats.** The labels are a first draft; see `bench/data/README.md`. The hard negatives were written as minimal pairs and the paraphrases as rewrites, which flatters the lexical guard. Every number here moves when the data does.

Rule (dev only): per (model, guard), the lowest threshold with **zero dev false hits**; then the combination with the best dev recall. Test is read once.

![precision and recall vs threshold, with and without the lexical guard](cache-eval.svg)

## Result

**Chosen: `nomic + 'clustering: '`, guard ≤2 words, threshold 0.895.** On test: hit rate on true repeats **86%** (30/35), false hits **3 of 33 served (9%)**. Zero false hits on dev became **3 of 33 (9%) on test**: 81 dev pairs were too few to expose them. Treat this threshold as provisional until the eval set is bigger.

Without the guard no model gets a usable hit rate at zero false hits: direction swaps ("5 km to miles" / "5 miles to km") score higher than most real paraphrases. Embeddings encode topic, and those pairs share it exactly.

## Where `nomic + 'clustering: '` goes wrong

Most similar hard negatives (embedding alone would serve the wrong cached answer):

- 0.997: "Convert 5 kilometers to miles." / "Convert 5 miles to kilometers."
- 0.997: "Convert 100 Fahrenheit to Celsius." / "Convert 100 Celsius to Fahrenheit."
- 0.994: "How do I convert a string to an integer in JavaScript?" / "How do I convert an integer to a string in JavaScript?"
- 0.980: "How do I enable dark mode on iPhone?" / "How do I disable dark mode on iPhone?"
- 0.979: "How many days are in a leap year?" / "How many days are in a non-leap year?"

Test false hits at the chosen setting:

- 0.930: "How do I center a div in CSS?" / "How do I center text in CSS?"
- 0.910: "What is 15% of 200?" / "What is 20% of 150?"
- 0.903: "What is the Great Wall of China?" / "How long is the Great Wall of China?"

Hardest test misses (real repeats the cache won't catch):

- 0.885: "Recommend a good book on habits." / "Suggest a good book about building habits."

- 0.890: "How do I read a CSV file in pandas?" / "Load a CSV into a pandas DataFrame, how?"
